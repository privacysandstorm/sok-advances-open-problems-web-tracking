The only artifact of our work is the thematic organization of the literature on Web Tracking. This artifact comprises of papers published at one of the top seven security and privacy conferences between 2005-2024 along with the topics related to web tracking assigned to them as explained in~\autoref{sec:methodology}. It is made available at \href{https://osf.io/zsy7e/overview?view_only=a346b98bf89244b5b17d842f6fb3fb13}{this URL}.

Additionally, an extended and live version of this SoK exists at this URL (\textit{anonymized for submission}) to provide more detailed explanations about the different concepts covered in the concise submitted version of the SoK. This longer version takes the form of an open-source software repository that contains the \LaTeX{} source files and scripts to automatically generate and publish the documents. We intend to maintain and update on a regular basis this extended version of the SoK to incorporate ecosystem changes, new findings related to web tracking, and other suggestions from the community that can be made through issues or pull requests.
